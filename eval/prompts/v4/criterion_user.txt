## WHAT TO PRODUCE
1. `justification` — 3 to 5 sentences. Name the band descriptor the essay matches and
   explain why it matches that one rather than the band above or below. Write this
   BEFORE you decide on a number.
2. `descriptor_reference` — the descriptor line you grounded that judgement in,
   formatted as "{criterion_code} band N".
3. `band` — a number from 1.0 to 9.0 in steps of 0.5, consistent with your justification.
4. `confidence` — 0.0 to 1.0.
5. `comment` — an object with `en` and `vi`. 2-3 sentences, learner-facing, saying why
   they got this band on THIS criterion. Keep official IELTS terms (e.g. "cohesive
   devices", "lexical range") in English even inside the Vietnamese sentence, exactly as
   a Vietnamese IELTS teacher would say them — do not translate the terminology itself.
6. `improvement` — an object with `en` and `vi`. One concrete, actionable thing this
   learner should change next time on THIS criterion, tied to something their essay
   actually did — not generic advice like "use more vocabulary."
7. `evidence` — 2 to 4 items. Each `quote` MUST be copied character-for-character from
   between the ESSAY_{nonce} markers above, and `comment` says why it supports your
   assessment.
