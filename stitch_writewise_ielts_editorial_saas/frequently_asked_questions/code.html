Getting Started

WriteWise is an advanced EdTech platform designed to help students improve their academic writing through instant, AI-driven feedback and editorial guidance.
It's tailored for high school and university students seeking to refine their essays, research papers, and analytical writing skills in a supportive, low-pressure environment.
Simply log in to your dashboard, click "New Essay," paste your text into the editor, and hit "Analyze." You'll receive comprehensive feedback within seconds.
